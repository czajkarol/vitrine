Curated and For you need the local index, so they are only available for the Art Institute.

How this works

Curated ranks every indexed artwork by a weighted score over six signals, all of them real fields from the museum's data.

    These are our heuristics about what suits a screen, not claims about art.

    For you re-ranks the Curated picks by what you have liked. It does not replace the quality score — a perfect match roughly doubles it.

    It counts the facets of your favourites: what the picture is of weighs most, then its culture and period, then the kind of object.

    Only what you press counts — likes, dislikes and hides. Nothing is read from what you skip or how long you look.
